# Golf Putt · 迷你高尔夫

Free single-file HTML5 mini golf game. 9 handcrafted holes with sand traps,
water hazards (+1 stroke penalty), slope zones and a lip-out cup — only slow
putts drop in. One HTML file, zero assets.

**Play:** https://shuangyueliao.github.io/golf-putt/

- Drag back from the ball and release to putt (mouse / touch);
  arrow keys + hold Space to charge on desktop
- Wall bounces keep 85% energy, exponential friction, sub-stepped physics
- Procedural canvas graphics and WebAudio sounds, no dependencies
- Best round saved locally, full scorecard after hole 9
